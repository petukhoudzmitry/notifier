Live Queue Registration Closed

5/5/2026

As previously announced by the Embassy of Italy on 15 April 2026, registration for the TLScontact waiting list (“Live queue”) was open until 30 April 2026.

  • After 30 April 2026, no new applications are accepted.

END OF APPOINTMENT REQUESTS FROM RELATIVES OF EU CITIZENS

4/29/2026

Please note that TLScontact will finish accepting appointment requests from relatives of EU citizens or Belarusian citizens residing in Italy on April 30, 2026, as part of the transition to the new service provider for Italian visa application processing.

For requests received before that date, TLScontact will book appointments until June 30, 2026, subject to availability.

Unserved requests will then be transferred to the Embassy of Italy for processing by the new service provider, and applicants will be notified via email.

From May 1st 2026, requests for priority appointments should be sent to the new supplier only, as explained on the Embassy website .

TRANSFER OF ITALIAN VISA PROCESSING TO NEW PROVIDER: PROCEDURE

4/22/2026

As recently announced by the Italian Embassy in Minsk , outsourcing of Italian visa applications will transfer from TLScontact to a new service provider from July 1st, 2026.

As part of the transition process:

  • TLScontact will continue to receive Italian visa applicants until June 30, 2026.
  • Applicants will be able to register on the TLScontact appointment waiting list (“live queue”) until April 30, 2026. After that date, any non-verified requests will be transferred to the Italian Embassy for processing by the new service provider, under their procedures.
  • After the closure of its Italian visa centre, TLScontact will transfer all applicant data to the Embassy of Italy, the Personal Data Controller, for processing by the new supplier at the discretion of the Embassy. All applicant data will then be deleted from TLScontact systems.

For more information, please consult the Embassy website .
